How It Works

Your Journey to a Perfect Smile

From your first consultation to your final reveal — here's exactly what to expect.

1

Consultation

A thorough examination including digital X-rays, photography, and an honest discussion of your goals, concerns, and budget.

2

Smile Design

Using digital smile design software and facial mapping, we create a preview of your planned result — so you can approve the outcome before treatment begins.

3

Treatment

Treatment is carried out by your named specialist in our fully equipped surgical suites, with comfort measures throughout. We keep you informed at every stage.

4

The Reveal

The moment your new smile is complete — we love this part as much as you do. Professional photographs are taken and you leave with a personalised aftercare plan.
