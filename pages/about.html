How It All Began

Founded on a Belief That Every Smile Deserves the Best

In 2003, Dr. Arjun Mehta returned from his postgraduate training in London with one clear goal: to bring genuinely world-class dental care to Chandigarh — without asking patients to compromise on either quality or comfort.

Starting with a modest four-chair clinic in Sector 22, SmileCare quickly earned a reputation for three things: clinical precision, patient empathy, and absolute transparency. Word spread quickly. Within five years, the clinic had expanded to a full floor with specialist rooms, a dedicated paediatric wing, and a surgical suite.

Today, SmileCare is home to more than 20 specialist dentists, a team of trained hygienists and dental nurses, and a dedicated patient care coordinator. We hold NABH accreditation, operate three state-of-the-art surgical suites, and serve patients from across India, the UK, Canada, Australia, and the Middle East.

But the founding principle remains unchanged: you walk in anxious, you walk out smiling.

Our Journey

Two Decades of Growth

Key milestones in SmileCare's story — from a four-chair start-up to a nationally recognised centre of dental excellence.

2003 — Founded

Dr. Arjun Mehta opens SmileCare in Sector 22 with four treatment chairs, a shared OPG machine, and one simple promise: no patient leaves in pain or confusion.

2003
2010

2010 — Expansion & NABH

Expanded to a full-floor, 14-chair multi-speciality facility. Achieved NABH accreditation — becoming one of Chandigarh's first dental clinics to receive the prestigious quality certification.

2018 — Digital Dentistry

Installed North India's first in-clinic CEREC same-day crown milling system. Launched the dedicated dental tourism programme, welcoming patients from 12 countries in the first year.

2024 — 15,000 Patients & Beyond

Celebrated our 15,000th patient, opened a dedicated paediatric wing, and launched Saturday extended-hours and Sunday morning clinics to make expert care even more accessible.
