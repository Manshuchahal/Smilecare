The Case for India

Why Thousands Choose India for Dental Care

Outstanding clinical quality, dramatic cost savings, and a destination worth visiting — India has become the world's top dental tourism destination.

💰

60–80% Cost Savings

Dental treatment in India costs a fraction of what you'd pay in the UK, USA, Canada, or Australia — without any reduction in materials quality, sterilisation standards, or clinical expertise. A single dental implant that costs £2,500 in London costs ₹35,000–₹45,000 (approximately £330–£420) at SmileCare.

🏅

NABH Accredited Quality

SmileCare holds NABH accreditation — India's equivalent of the UK's CQC or the USA's Joint Commission. Our sterilisation protocols, materials specifications, and clinical governance standards meet international benchmarks. We use the same implant brands (Straumann, Nobel Biocare) as leading European clinics.

✈️

Easy to Reach

Chandigarh International Airport has direct and one-stop connections from London, Dubai, Singapore, Toronto, and Sydney. The city is clean, safe, and welcoming — with excellent hotels, restaurants, and cultural attractions. Many patients combine treatment with a holiday to Himachal Pradesh or Rajasthan.

🌍

English-Speaking Doctors

All SmileCare specialists are fluent English speakers, trained in India and abroad. Our international patient coordinator handles all logistics — from initial consultation by video call through to airport pick-up, hotel arrangements, and post-treatment documentation for your dentist back home.

⚡

No Waiting Lists

In the UK, NHS dental waiting lists can run to 18 months. In the USA, scheduling with specialists often requires weeks of lead time. At SmileCare, international patients can typically be seen within 48 hours of arrival, with complex multi-implant cases completed within 5–7 days.

📋

Full Medical Documentation

We provide complete medical records, X-rays, and treatment summaries in a format compatible with European and American dental records systems. Many of our patients' home dentists have commented positively on the quality of work they've found at review appointments.
