Our Story

Chandigarh's Most Trusted Dental Clinic Since 2003

Founded by Dr. Arjun Mehta, SmileCare began with a simple conviction: that every person deserves exceptional dental care delivered in a warm, anxiety-free environment. Two decades on, that commitment drives everything we do.

From our state-of-the-art clinic in Sector 22, our team of 20+ specialists serves patients from across India and abroad — combining the latest in digital dentistry with a deeply personal approach to care.

  • NABH-accredited facility with the highest safety standards
  • Digital OPG and 3D CBCT for precision diagnostics
  • Painless dentistry with conscious sedation options
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs, ever
  • EMI options and corporate tie-ups available
